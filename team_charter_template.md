## Purpose of the Charter
This charter helps your research team set expectations before the work gets heavy. In science, teamwork is not just about dividing tasks. It is about communicating clearly, documenting decisions, holding one another accountable, and solving problems before they become disasters.

Complete this document together. Do not split the questions up and answer them separately. The point is the conversation.

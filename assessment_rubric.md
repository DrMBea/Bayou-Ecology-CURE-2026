# Assessment Rubric: Ecology CURE Project

## Course
**ECOL 404B Ecology**  
**Project:** Where Bayous Meet Bytes - Ecology CURE  
**Total Points:** 100

## Purpose
This rubric assesses the full CURE experience, including ecological reasoning, data exploration, teamwork, scientific communication, and responsible use of digital tools. The goal is not perfection. The goal is evidence that students are thinking, investigating, documenting, and communicating like developing scientists.

| Criteria | Excellent | Proficient | Developing | Needs Revision | Points |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Research Question and Ecological Focus | Research question is clear, focused, and strongly connected to Louisiana ecology or environmental change. | Research question is clear and connected to ecology, but may need slight narrowing. | Research question is present but too broad, vague, or weakly connected to ecology. | Research question is missing, unclear, or not ecological. | 15 |
| Use of Data and Documentation | Dataset is used appropriately; variables are clearly described; data decisions are documented. | Dataset is used with mostly clear variable descriptions and some documentation. | Dataset use is incomplete or documentation is limited. | Data use is inaccurate, undocumented, or missing. | 15 |
| Visualization and Analysis | Visualization is accurate, labeled, and supports meaningful interpretation. | Visualization is mostly accurate and supports interpretation. | Visualization is present but unclear, mislabeled, or weakly connected to the question. | Visualization is missing or does not match the data. | 15 |
| Ecological Interpretation | Interpretation connects evidence to ecological concepts with clear reasoning and appropriate caution. | Interpretation connects evidence to ecology but may need more depth or caution. | Interpretation is basic, descriptive, or weakly connected to ecological concepts. | Interpretation is unsupported, inaccurate, or missing. | 20 |
| Scientific Communication | Final product is organized, professional, clear, and appropriate for a scientific audience. | Final product is understandable and mostly organized. | Final product communicates some ideas but lacks clarity, structure, or polish. | Final product is incomplete or difficult to follow. | 15 |
| Teamwork and Charter Use | Team used the charter actively; roles, communication, and contributions are clearly documented. | Team used the charter with minor gaps in documentation or follow-through. | Team completed the charter but used it inconsistently. | Charter is missing or not connected to the project. | 10 |
| Responsible AI / Digital Tool Use | Tool use is transparent, ethical, verified, and properly disclosed. | Tool use is disclosed with minor gaps in explanation. | Tool use is unclear or disclosure is incomplete. | Tool use is undisclosed, inappropriate, or unverifiable. | 5 |
| Reflection and Growth | Reflection shows honest insight into research process, challenges, learning, and next steps. | Reflection explains learning and challenges with some detail. | Reflection is brief, general, or surface-level. | Reflection is missing or does not address the prompt. | 5 |

## Total: 100 Points

### Recommended Grade Interpretation
- **90-100:** Strong ecological research performance and communication
- **80-89:** Solid work with minor gaps in depth, clarity, or documentation
- **70-79:** Basic completion with areas needing stronger interpretation or organization
- **60-69:** Incomplete or underdeveloped research process
- **Below 60:** Major missing components or limited evidence of CURE engagement

## Instructor Feedback Priorities
When grading, prioritize:

1. Ecological reasoning over technical perfection.
2. Honest documentation over pretending the process was flawless.
3. Clear interpretation over fancy visuals.
4. Responsible tool use over unsupported AI-generated claims.
5. Growth, revision, and teamwork as part of the research process.
